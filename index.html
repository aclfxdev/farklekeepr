Frequently asked

Does FarkleKeepr roll dice for me?
No - you play with your own physical dice. FarkleKeepr just tracks scores, turns, and special combinations.

Can I play across multiple devices?
Yes - create a shared game and share the join code with other players, each on their own phone.

What does Premium Edition unlock?
Cross-device shared games, plus additional app themes and icons.
